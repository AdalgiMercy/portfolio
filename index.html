Kanda Ops

System architect · 2026

6 agents5 LLM providers

A permanent agentic team that runs and grows Kanda — prospecting, content, follow-ups, and a daily executive briefing — so a solo founder stays on top of everything.

Read the case study

Problem

As a solo founder, I couldn't keep marketing, sales operations, and content running in parallel while also building the product — and hiring wasn't an option yet.

What I built

  • Six specialized agents, each with a distinct remit and voice: a Chef de Cabinet that synthesizes all open work into one daily plan; a Gardien that fact-checks every document against a single source of truth; plus Marketing Director, Sales Director, Content Writer, and Prospecting Writer.
  • A multi-provider LLM router across 5 providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, ZAI, MiMo) with automatic fallback on failure and per-run token + cost tracking.
  • A guardrail layer: a facts.yml single source of truth the Gardien audits (literal + semantic passes) to catch contradictions before anything ships.
  • Daily orchestration: a 6 AM cron runs Gardien → Marketing → Chef de Cabinet, producing one Telegram briefing — "here is your day, in order."
  • A SQLite state store for briefs, proposals, drafts, the prospect pipeline, the follow-up schedule, and run/cost history.

Impact

A solo founder runs marketing, sales ops, and content with a self-coordinating agent team; factual drift is caught automatically; and LLM cost is observable across providers — in production, with daily runs.

Kanda OS

Founder & sole engineer · 2025–2026

66 features 122 data models

A multi-tenant SaaS school-management platform for K-12 schools and higher-ed institutes across Central Africa and Senegal — academics, grades, payments, HR, mobile money, and more.

Read the case study

Problem

Schools in the region managed academics, fees, and communication across spreadsheets and paper — no affordable, offline-capable, all-in-one platform fit their reality.

What I built

  • 66 shipped features across 15+ API modules — academics, grading, report cards, billing, HR, campus/higher-ed, attendance, and scheduling.
  • 122 tenant-scoped data models behind centralized multi-tenant isolation (request-scoped tenant context injected automatically).
  • Flutterwave mobile-money integration (MTN/Orange/Wave) with webhook idempotency, server-side amount computation, and transactional payouts to schools.
  • A 45-finding security hardening: transactional payments (FOR UPDATE locks, anti-double-credit), IDOR fixes, JWT token-generation counters, and HMAC receipt signatures.
  • Blue-green Docker deploys with Nginx, PgBouncer, Postgres 16, and Redis; PWA with offline support.

A complete, production multi-tenant SaaS — built and operated solo end-to-end, from schema to deploy.
